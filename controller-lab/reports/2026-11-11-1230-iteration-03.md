## Technical rationale
The fractional accumulator must preserve the integral of mouse velocity across frames while avoiding floating-point quantization artifacts. The epsilon snapping only applies at distance <= 1e-12 from an integer, far below intentional subpixel motion. SDL3 relative motion uses floating-point deltas: https://wiki.libsdl.org/SDL3/SDL_MouseMotionEvent .
